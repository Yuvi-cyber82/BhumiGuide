BhumiGuide Intelligence

Last updated: 1 November 2026

BhumiGuide Intelligence is a final-year academic project built by students of G H Raisoni University, Amravati. It is not a commercial service, not a law firm, and not a government website.

1. What this site is

The site assembles land-purchase information that Indian state governments already publish — the buying procedure, the documents a state asks for, indicative stamp duty and registration charges, and the offices involved — and presents it for the district you choose, in nine Indian languages.

2. This is not legal or financial advice

Everything shown is general guidance for study and orientation. It is not legal advice, not a valuation, and not a substitute for a lawyer, a registered document writer or the concerned government office. Before you part with money or sign anything, confirm the facts with the Sub-Registrar, Tehsildar or municipal office that actually handles your plot.

3. Accuracy, and what we do not claim

Rules, rates and document names change, and they differ by state and sometimes by district. Each rule in this dataset carries the government source it was taken from, so you can check it yourself. Where no official source could be confirmed, the project says so openly rather than filling the gap in.

The charge calculator gives an indicative estimate, not a demand note. The document readiness check matches file names, not the contents of a file, so it can be wrong in both directions.
