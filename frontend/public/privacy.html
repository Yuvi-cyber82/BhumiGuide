Privacy Policy

BhumiGuide Intelligence

Last updated: 1 November 2026

BhumiGuide Intelligence is a final-year academic project built by students of G H Raisoni University, Amravati. It is not a commercial service and not a government website.

Who runs this site

The site is operated by a student project team at the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, G H Raisoni University, Amravati, India, under the guidance of Mr. Ashish Trivedi. Questions about this policy can be sent to yuvrajnaktode11@gmail.com.

What we collect

You can read most of the site without an account. If you create one, we store:

  • Your name and email address.
  • A hashed form of your password. The password itself is never stored.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account id, name, email address and profile picture, as supplied by Google. We request nothing beyond that, and we never receive your Google password.
  • Your progress in the app — which procedure steps you have ticked off, which documents you have checked, the answers you gave the Smart Procedure questionnaire, and your notification preferences.
  • Files you upload to the document readiness check, along with their file name, type and size.

Why we collect it

Only to make the site work: to sign you in, to show your saved progress when you return, and to tell you which documents you still appear to be missing. We do not profile you, we do not advertise, and we run no analytics or tracking scripts.

Who it is shared with

We do not sell or share your data. It is held by the services the site runs on:

  • MongoDB Atlas — the database (Mumbai region).
  • Render — the server.
  • Vercel — the website.
  • Groq — only the text of a question you type into the AI Assistant, so it can be answered. Your name, email and uploaded files are never sent there.

Uploaded documents

Files you upload are stored on the server's disk. That disk is temporary: it is erased whenever the free-tier server restarts, so uploaded files should be treated as short-lived. Please do not upload anything you are not willing to lose, and do not upload documents belonging to other people without their permission.

How long we keep it

Account and progress data stay until you ask us to delete them. Email us and we will remove your account and everything attached to it.

Your choices

  • You can use the whole guidance side of the site without signing in.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask for it to be deleted, by email.
  • You can revoke the site's access to your Google account at any time from Google Account permissions.

Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS and passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. This is a student project, however, and it has not been through a professional security review. Please keep that in mind before entrusting it with anything sensitive.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
